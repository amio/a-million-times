# Official Sources and Coverage

Research date: 2026-09-07. Images and videos belong to Humans since 1982 and their respective photographers; they are retained here for research and comparison. Reconstruction names are descriptive labels created by this project.

This research collected 22 official website pages, 28 deduplicated video files, and 76 product image URLs, and indexed 32 regular videos and 27 Shorts from the YouTube channel. **An indexed item has not necessarily been watched in full; inspection status is recorded below.**

Every website video was inspected through contact sheets sampled at 1–4-second intervals. Key rules received closer inspection through larger images and denser frame samples. Movements shorter than the sampling interval may still be missed. The 76 image URLs include alternate crops, materials, and installation photographs of the same compositions; they do not represent 76 different patterns. An additional attempt to access the official Moncler project page returned HTTP 429, so no choreography was inferred from it.

The three user-supplied videos correspond to V04, V12, and V27. Six supplied images supplement the time glyphs, column waves, and radial forms. The original files remain in the directory provided by the user.
